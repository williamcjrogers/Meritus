# Meritus data-source feasibility review

**Reviewed: 12 September 2026.** Scope: the sources and connector assumptions in `docs/compass_artifact_wf-940e6c99-1917-5535-9ade-03216d8ceabf_text_markdown.md`. This is a read-only source investigation, not a production ingestion test or a legal opinion. No credentials, subscriptions or outreach were used. The user confirms that QCS owns the software and has supplied its National Archives licence. That licence is not evidence of permission under the separate HMCTS court-list licensing scheme.

The feasible first release is company monitoring plus procurement notices and Payment Practices exports, supported by aggregate BSR intelligence. Court listings require a separate licensing decision. A named Gateway delay watchlist cannot be generated from the published BSR statistics alone. Commercial project data can fill some relationship gaps, subject to the purchased API scope and downstream-use rights.

## Material corrections to the strategy

| Proposition in the strategy | Verified correction and implementation consequence |
| --- | --- |
| TCC cause lists are a clean OGL API source. | Current CaTH policy separates ordinary reuse under Open Justice Licence v2.0 from computational reuse requiring an HMCTS Third-Party Courts and Tribunals Data Licence. Public visibility does not supply this permission. Disable automated court-list ingestion pending that grant. [HMCTS policy](https://www.court-tribunal-hearings.service.gov.uk/publishing-policy) |
| Payment Practices requires scraping because there is no API. | The service supplies a complete CSV export. Use this as the primary connector and follow report URLs only for information missing from the export. [Official export](https://check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk/export/) |
| BSR Gateway data can identify individual delayed projects. | The latest Gateway publication is a 22-page PDF of aggregate measures. The remediation workbook also aggregates counts, with disclosure controls intended to prevent identification of some buildings. Neither is a complete named application-status feed. [Gateway publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/building-safety-regulator-building-control-approval-application-data-june-to-august-2026), [remediation workbook](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a8d7b893b22fb169dc18d0e/Building_Safety_Remediation_Tables_July_2026.ods) |
| August 2025 was twelve months after the Innovation Unit started. | The latest BSR PDF says August **2026** marked twelve months. Its 43-week/39% figures are the earlier baseline; the June to August 2026 headline figures are 22 weeks/92% for new builds and 33 weeks/89% for external remediation. Categories, periods and denominators must accompany any comparison. [BSR PDF, pages 3 and 6](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a9a852a5a0c25165ae468ee/Building_Safety_Regulator_building_control_approval_application_data_June_to_August_2026.pdf) |
| Gazette structured access is paid-only, and its data cannot be redistributed. | Public feeds and notice representations exist. Non-personal Gazette information is generally OGL; the paid delivery service has additional contract restrictions. Automated public access is constrained by fair-use rules and robots directives. [Reuse](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/data), [formats](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/data/formats), [service terms](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/data/terms-and-conditions) |
| Both procurement sources expose precisely OCDS 1.1.5. | Live packages from both endpoints reported `version: "1.1"`, with different extension arrays. Preserve the declared version and extensions. Do not validate against a bare standard schema and discard UK-specific fields. [Find a Tender documentation](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation/1.0/GET-ocdsReleasePackages), [Contracts Finder documentation](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation/Notices/1/GET-Published-Notice-OCDS-Search) |
| Companies House can be mirrored by opening every stream. | Its published streaming limit is two concurrent connections per account. New connections close the oldest beyond that limit. A watchlist with selected streams and REST reconciliation is a practical initial design. [Streaming guide](https://developer-specs.company-information.service.gov.uk/streaming-api/guides/overview) |
| OGL makes all source content unrestricted, and selling raw public data is necessarily forbidden. | OGL reuse and commercial-feed access are different questions. Companies House imposes no additional rules on use of register information, while users remain responsible for applicable law. Personal data, third-party material and contractual restrictions need separate treatment. [Companies House data products](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/companies-house-data-products) |

## Connector contracts

These are proposed implementation contracts derived from the documentation, not claims that connectors have been built. Operational defaults below are engineering choices unless identified as source requirements.

### 1. Companies House

**Coverage and rights.** Company profile, filing history, officers, charges and insolvency data are available. These establish registered entities and statutory events, not project participation, directors' commercial job titles, or underlying claims. An officer resignation alone does not establish that the finance director departed. Auditors' resignation information may require interpreting filing types and documents. The public monthly CSV is a basic company snapshot, not a complete historical mirror of all resources. [Data products](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/companies-house-data-products), [API reference](https://developer-specs.company-information.service.gov.uk/streaming-api/reference)

- REST base: `https://api.company-information.service.gov.uk`; register an application and key. Relevant documented paths include `/company/{company_number}`, `/company/{company_number}/filing-history`, `/company/{company_number}/officers`, `/company/{company_number}/charges` and `/company/{company_number}/insolvency`. Confirm each resource's pagination against its own specification. [Developer hub](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/)
- Stream operations include `/companies`, `/filings`, `/insolvency-cases`, `/charges` and `/officers`; use the streaming authentication and server details, not the REST host by assumption. [Streaming reference](https://developer-specs.company-information.service.gov.uk/streaming-api/reference)
- Persist the stream's `event.timepoint` only after the raw event and normalised result are durable. Handle `changed` and `deleted`; preserve `resource_uri`, `resource_id`, `event.published_at` and raw data. Ignore blank heartbeat lines. Reconnect from the last successfully processed timepoint. A `416` means the retained replay window cannot serve the checkpoint; stop and reconcile rather than silently skipping. Maximum two concurrent connections per account; streaming `429` requires at least one minute before reconnecting. [Streaming guide](https://developer-specs.company-information.service.gov.uk/streaming-api/guides/overview), [event schema](https://developer-specs.company-information.service.gov.uk/streaming-api/resources/insolvencycasesstream)
- REST limit: 600 requests per five-minute period. The current guideline documents `429` until the period resets; it does **not** guarantee the strategy's claimed REST `Retry-After` header. Honour it if supplied and otherwise back off conservatively. [Rate limits](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/developer-guidelines)

**Initial scope:** preserve company numbers as strings, including leading zeroes and jurisdiction prefixes. Poll a curated watchlist, or use one or two relevant streams, with periodic REST reconciliation. Do not claim complete histories from the basic snapshot.

### 2. Find a Tender

**Verified unauthenticated endpoint:** `GET https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/api/1.0/ocdsReleasePackages`.

Parameters are `updatedFrom`, `updatedTo`, `limit` (1 to 100), `cursor` and optional `stages`. The dates are documented as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS`; returned releases can include explicit offsets. Follow returned `links.next` without decoding or constructing cursor values. The API documents `429` and `503` with `Retry-After`. It does not publish a fixed requests-per-second allowance on this page. Release IDs are unique within an OCID; party IDs are unique within a release. [API contract](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation/1.0/GET-ocdsReleasePackages)

**Proposed state:** fixed collection window, exact request URL, next URL, completed-window watermark, `(source, ocid, release.id)`, and content hash. Freeze the upper bound before page one, replay a small overlapping window, retain changed versions, then advance the watermark only when the whole window is committed. Omit `stages` initially to avoid losing new notice categories; identify notice types from the actual release and documents.

**Coverage:** notices can name buyers and suppliers, values, lots and planned periods. Preserve identifiers with their schemes, such as `GB-COH`, `GB-PPON` and source-specific identifiers. No Companies House match should be assumed from a name alone. Award or framework participation does not prove actual mobilisation, subcontractor involvement or practical completion.

**Live check:** an unauthenticated one-release window for 10 to 11 September 2026 returned HTTP 200, OGL v3.0, OCDS `1.1`, UK and performance-failures extensions, and `links.next`. The sample notice `086167-2026` contained GBP at tender level but AED at lot level. This is evidence of a validation requirement, not a measured population-wide error rate. [Sample notice](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/086167-2026)

**Performance notices:** the £5 million KPI rule has exceptions, including frameworks, private-utility contracts, concessions and light-touch contracts. Breach/poor-performance reporting is not confined to contracts above £5 million. A breach causing full termination is reported through a termination notice instead. A central performance register was still described as forthcoming in the July 2026 guidance. Monitor notices, including termination notices; do not invent a register API or treat every performance notice as adverse. [Current guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-manage-phase/guidance-contract-performance-notices-html)

### 3. Contracts Finder

**Verified unauthenticated endpoint:** `GET https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Published/Notices/OCDS/Search`.

Use `publishedFrom`, `publishedTo`, `limit` (1 to 100), `cursor`, and optional `stages` (`planning,tender,award,implementation`). Here publication date includes the latest edit and first publication in the current stage. The current documentation uses cursor pagination, despite older guidance describing page/size parameters. Its rate-limit response is documented as **403**, followed by a five-minute pause, rather than Find a Tender's `429` contract. [Endpoint documentation](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation/Notices/1/GET-Published-Notice-OCDS-Search)

A matching one-release live window returned HTTP 200, OCDS `1.1`, OGL v3.0 and `links.next`. The buyer and supplier carried source identifiers, not Companies House numbers. Use the same durable window/replay pattern as Find a Tender, but keep source cursors separate. The documented daily CSV alternative is `/Harvester/Notices/Data/CSV/{year}/{month}/{day}`. [API catalogue](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/apidocumentation)

**Coverage limitation:** this is procurement publication data. Repeated notices and overlap with Find a Tender do not constitute independent corroboration. Deduplicate provenance and contract links before counting signals. Planned contract dates are not verified practical-completion or retention-release dates.

### 4. Payment Practices

**Verified export:** `GET https://check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk/export/csv/`, linked from the official export page; returned HTTP 200 and `text/csv` without authentication. The landing page says it exports statistical and yes/no answers, with report URLs for further narrative. The current CSV additionally contains several narrative columns, so derive supported fields from the observed header and keep a fallback to report pages. [Export page](https://check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk/export/)

Observed fields include `Report Id`, `Policy Regime`, `Financial period start date`, `Start date`, `End date`, `Filing date`, `Company`, `Company number`, `Average time to pay`, `% Invoices not paid within agreed terms`, `% Invoices not paid due to dispute`, construction-retention fields and `URL`.

**Proposed state:** full snapshot checksum and retrieval time; row key `Report Id`; row content hash; original report URL; reporting-period and policy-regime fields. Diff each complete export against the previous successful snapshot to find additions and corrections. Do not assume report IDs or filing dates form a reliable modification cursor. Parse quoted CSV properly, including embedded newlines. Preserve blank/not-applicable values rather than converting them to zero. No API rate limit or incremental cursor was verified; daily or weekly snapshot retrieval is sufficient for twice-yearly reporting.

**Corrections and coverage:** retention reporting applies to financial years beginning on or after **1 April 2025**, not 1 January 2025. The £54 million/£27 million/250 thresholds require the detailed qualifying-year and group rules, not a simple current-size test. Reports describe the reporting business, not each project or counterparty. Changes in reporting regimes and periods affect comparability. [DBT reporting guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/business-payment-practices-and-performance-reporting-requirements/duty-to-report-guidance-to-reporting-on-payment-practices-and-performance)

### 5. TCC cause lists through HMCTS CaTH

**Status: licence-gated for this proposed use.** The live publishing policy covers computational analysis of both public and enhanced data. It also controls onward sharing. Enhanced-data restrictions include profiling/re-identification and combinations creating new risks. A generic OGL footer does not override the specific policy. [Publishing policy](https://www.court-tribunal-hearings.service.gov.uk/publishing-policy)

The application guidance, published 15 July 2026, requires a completed DPIA and compatibility with CaTH's API requirements. Additional policy/transparency documents may be required. Successful applicants receive further access information. Therefore no unrestricted public API endpoint, quota, retention period or replay cursor is asserted here. [Application route](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-hmcts-third-party-courts-and-tribunals-data-licence)

**Proposed contract after approval:** bind ingestion to the approved purpose, permitted datasets, retention/deletion schedule and onward-sharing terms. Record list publication time, hearing date, source document identity/version, court, case reference, party strings and source URL. Handle amended/cancelled listings. Establish actual API schema and correction/removal handling during onboarding. A listing proves a scheduled proceeding, not its merits, outcome, claim value, expert instruction or entitlement. Company matching must permit unresolved names. QCS's National Archives licence must not be used as the permission flag for HMCTS.

### 6. The Gazette

**Public interface:** documented notice-feed URL `https://www.thegazette.co.uk/insolvency/notice/data.feed`; JSON alternative `/insolvency/notice/data.json`. Supported filters include `noticetype`, `start-publish-date`, `end-publish-date`, `results-page`, `results-page-size` and `sort-by`. Atom/JSON supplies pagination links and notice identifiers. Use exact publisher taxonomy and separate petitions, appointments, resolutions and outcomes. [Publisher's feed specification](https://github.com/TheGazette/DevDocs/blob/master/notice/notice-feed.md)

**Important access constraint:** fair-use rules require automated access between 21:00 and 07:00 UK time, no more than five requests per ten seconds, an identifiable application/contact User-Agent and compliance with robots. Live `robots.txt` adds `Crawl-delay: 10` and disallows the per-notice `data.xml`, `data.pdf`, `data.rdf`, `data.ttl` and `data.jsonld` paths. Do not interpret the existence of those formats as permission to harvest them, or use alternate paths to evade restrictions. A conservative compliant collector would observe at least ten seconds between requests and use permitted feed/HTML routes; clarify or contract for high-volume structured delivery. [Fair use](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/fair-use-policy), [robots](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/robots.txt)

**Rights:** the open content licence excludes personal data. The paid service restricts access to the contracted customer and contains attribution, preservation and end-user provisions. It is inaccurate to collapse these into either unrestricted resale or a blanket prohibition on all Gazette-derived redistribution. Review the actual order sheet for the intended product. [Reuse statement](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/data), [paid terms, clauses 3.6 and 5](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/data/terms-and-conditions)

**Proposed state:** bounded publication-date window with overlap, returned next link, notice ID, content hash and last verification time. The feed is publication-date searchable; do not assume it supplies a complete change/deletion log. Preserve notice status and correction evidence. Companies House number extraction can support company matching, but the Gazette does not establish the affected company's project or contractual supply chain.

### 7. BSR and remediation publications

**Gateway:** poll the official collection for new publication links, retain publication date, reporting window, category and source attachment hash. The current June to August 2026 source is PDF only on its GOV.UK landing page. Store extracted aggregate measurements with page/table references and methodology. Treat revisions as new source versions. No project-level Gateway application API, statutory-expiry cursor or applicant-company list was verified. [Collection](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/building-safety-regulator-building-control-approval-application-data), [current publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/building-safety-regulator-building-control-approval-application-data-june-to-august-2026)

**Remediation:** the July 2026 publication has an ODS workbook, published on 26 August 2026. It contains programme, tenure, local-authority, developer and other aggregate tables. Its Notes tab expressly describes suppression/banding to prevent identification of buildings in certain areas. Tables can overlap across programmes; the workbook points to a combined table to avoid double counting. A named developer progress comparison is not a named building/freeholder register. Use scheme-specific keys and retain notes alongside metrics. [Publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/building-safety-remediation-monthly-data-release-july-2026), [downloaded workbook](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a8d7b893b22fb169dc18d0e/Building_Safety_Remediation_Tables_July_2026.ods)

**Consequence:** BSR supports market sizing and contextual benchmarks. A project delay signal requires separate, lawfully obtained project/application evidence. A difference between a median and a statutory determination period does not itself establish compensable project delay, causation or contractual allocation.

### 8. Commercial construction and credit data

**Barbour ABI:** a documented REST API exists at `https://api.barbour-abi.com/v4`, requiring a subscription with a Developer or Locations licence and API credentials. It exposes projects, companies, project roles, fields/lookups and merge records. Search uses `limit`/`offset`, default 50/0, maximum limit 500, and a 10,000-record pagination ceiling; partition larger sets using filters such as `project_last_published`. Synchronise `/projects/merges` and `/companies/merges`. No numeric sustained rate allowance was verified. The documentation's forward-looking rate-limit wording sits awkwardly beside the v4 entry point, so confirm the contracted runtime limits. [API documentation](https://barbour-abi.com/api-fundamentals/)

The retrieved client-terms page explicitly applies to contracts entered between 16 October 2024 and 11 May 2026; it is insufficient to establish September 2026 purchase terms. It restricts copying and third-party distribution. Obtain the applicable contract before committing to database ingestion, derived publications, cross-company access or export retention. [Dated client terms](https://barbour-abi.com/client-terms-and-conditions/)

**Glenigan:** the official product page confirms REST integration for construction leads and project intelligence. Its current general terms, clauses 7.10 and 8.2, limit use to internal purposes and restrict third-party disclosure, competing products and statistical information supplied to third parties. A free external digest or index can therefore require permission even if raw rows are omitted. Do not assume that buying a seat includes API or publication rights. No authenticated calls, subscription quota, field completeness, replay guarantee or price were verified. [API product](https://www.glenigan.com/our-products/construction-leads-api/), [current terms](https://www.glenigan.com/general-terms-of-business/)

**Creditsafe:** official packages confirm a commercial Connect REST API and company monitoring. That establishes a credible integration option, not a free distress feed or permission to redistribute scores. Use its current API catalogue and the contracted UK report/monitoring entitlement before specifying company-matching, event types, quotas and retention. The September 2026 changelog itself records a corrected company-search response schema. [Packages](https://www.creditsafe.com/gb/en/more/packages/packages.html), [API changelog](https://doc.creditsafe.com/connect-apis-catalog/changelog)

Experian, Red Flag Alert and the remaining named news/job sources were not subjected to authenticated or contract-level verification in this review. Keep them as optional, unverified connectors. The quoted Glenigan/Barbour annual price bands are not verified official prices.

## Common acceptance conditions

1. A connector is operational only when its access method, allowed purpose, attribution, onward-sharing rights, retention rules and actual schema are recorded. A source name or marketing page is insufficient.
2. Retain the source record and exact URL, retrieval time, source date, payload hash, parser version and any source correction. Advance ingestion state only after durable persistence.
3. Keep organisation, procurement, contract, lot, building and project identifiers distinct. Model relationships with evidence and confidence; preserve unresolved matches. Do not make every entity a Companies House company.
4. Separate observation from inference: for example, “petition notice published”, “payment report worsened” and “contract period amended” are observations. “In financial distress”, “likely to dispute” and “compensable Gateway delay” are assessments requiring further evidence.
5. Related republications and consequences of the same underlying event are not independent signals. Require source lineage and analyst review before publishing or contacting named organisations.

**Verification boundary:** Companies House authenticated endpoints and streaming continuity were documented but not exercised. Find a Tender, Contracts Finder and the Payment Practices export received live unauthenticated HTTP 200 checks. Gazette format/filter contracts were documented; no bulk Gazette crawl was performed. The BSR PDF and remediation ODS were inspected. Commercial access was assessed from public primary documentation only. These findings support a scoped implementation, not claims of production completeness or predictive accuracy.
